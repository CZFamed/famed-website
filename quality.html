Quality Control

Quality in a foundry is decided before the pour. Our control points start with the incoming scrap and the pattern, not with the final inspection.

Quality system

Our management system is certified to GB/T 19001-2016 / ISO 9001:2015. The certificate covers the design and manufacture of castings and machined parts, and is currently valid.

  • GB/T 19001-2016 / ISO 9001:2015
  • Covering design and manufacture of castings and machined parts
  • Currently valid; copy available on request

Control points

01

Incoming materials

Charge materials and alloys are verified on arrival; scrap returns are sorted by grade to keep chemistry predictable.

02

Pattern and coating

Foam patterns are measured and assembled against the tool drawing; coating thickness and drying are controlled before moulding.

03

Chemistry

Every heat is checked on the optical emission spectrometer, with the result recorded against the heat number.

04

Pouring

Pouring temperature and time are logged per mould; inoculation is controlled for ductile iron.

05

Dimensional & mechanical

CMM and calliper checks for machined features; hardness on every batch and tensile or impact tests where the drawing calls for them.

06

Final & documentation

Surface, finish and marking are checked at packing, and a material certificate accompanies every shipment.

Testing equipment

Equipment list is indicative of current capability; the on-site register is available for verification during supplier audits.

  • Optical emission spectrometer
  • ZEISS bridge-type coordinate measuring machine (CMM)
  • Handheld 3D laser scanning for pattern and part verification
  • Hardness testers (Brinell / portable)
  • Non-destructive testing (dye penetrant / ultrasonic)
  • Tensile testing machine
  • Low-temperature impact testing
  • Metallographic and laboratory facilities

Standards we work to

Material grades
GB/T 9439 (grey iron), GB/T 1348 (ductile iron), equivalent ISO and ASTM grades on request
Castings
GB/T 6414 dimensional tolerances; ISO 8062 for general tolerances
Machining
General tolerances to GB/T 1804-m unless the drawing specifies tighter
Flanges
EN 1092, ANSI B16.5, JIS and GB flange standards
Documents
Material certificate, dimensional report, hardness record, packing list
